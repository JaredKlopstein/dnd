# Dicebound

A playable crash course in **Dungeons & Dragons 5th Edition**. Learn the real rules by playing a short fantasy adventure (about 30–45 minutes).

## How to play

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. Nothing to install. Your progress saves at the start of each chapter.

## What you'll learn

| Chapter | You learn |
| --- | --- |
| 1. The Table | What D&D is, the DM and players, the core loop, dice and dice notation |
| 2. Who Are You? | Species, class, background, ability scores and modifiers, HP, AC, proficiency |
| 3. The Wobbly Wyvern | Roleplay, ability checks, skills, DCs, failing forward, contests |
| 4. The Whispering Wood | Advantage and disadvantage, the Help action, darkvision, passive Perception, saving throws |
| 5. Ambush! | Initiative, turns, movement, actions, bonus actions, attack and damage rolls, crits, reactions, opportunity attacks, 0 HP and death saves, short rests |
| 6. Crookback Mine | Solving problems without fighting, group checks, riddles |
| 7. The Cheese Throne | Boss fight: multiattack, monster reactions, creative actions, getting enemies to surrender |
| Epilogue | XP and leveling up, plus a cheat sheet for your first real session |

There's also a **Rulebook** that unlocks pages as you learn, a live **Character Sheet**, achievements, and pop quizzes.

Play as a Fighter, Rogue, Wizard, or Cleric. Each class plays differently in combat.
